## Conditions and interpretation

- Figures 4–10 compare open-loop hybrid and baseline digital simulations at six conditions. The panels use sea-level standard-day static, 9,144 m at Mach 1.8, 12,192 m at Mach 0.4, 13,716 m at Mach 2.5, and 22,555 m at Mach 1.5 and 2.5. See the per-panel captions for the conditions and each ordinate.
- Figure 11 adds experimental data at sea level, static, 297.9 K. Its abscissa is the fraction of design main-combustor fuel flow. Figure 12 shows the augmented case at sea level, static, 302.8 K. Its abscissa is the fraction of design maximum augmentor fuel flow.
- Figures 13–15 compare simulated time histories at sea level and static conditions. Figures 13 and 14 indicate that idle-area reset was on.
- Figures 16–20 use normalized axes or parameter forms specific to the report. In particular, the turbine enthalpy-drop parameter must be interpreted using Appendix B before comparing it with an efficiency map. Appendix C's program reads component data cards and scale factors. The figures supply plotted curves, and the listing describes their use.

The complete model equations are in Appendix B, printed pp. 13–26. Appendix C, printed pp. 27–38, has the digital program. Appendix D, printed pp. 39–51, has unnumbered analog patching diagrams and potentiometer settings. Table I, printed p. 53, lists published model constants including volumes, shaft inertias, and selected loss terms.
